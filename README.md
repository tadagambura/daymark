# Daymark

**Countdown to anything, and never miss a birthday**

> #14 of 31 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Daily glance · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Plans, Trips & Occasions

## What it is

Beautiful countdowns (and 'days since' counters) for the things you're looking forward to: holidays, payday, exams, moving day, concerts. Plus a people & birthdays mode: the people you buy for, their birthdays and anniversaries, a running note of gift ideas for each, and a nudge two weeks before with your ideas and budget ready. Lives on your home screen as a widget.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Countdown apps get steady downloads, are quick to build and sell well as a lifetime unlock. Birthdays are just countdowns to people, so folding Daymark in gives Daymark several guaranteed return visits a year, a huge Christmas peak, and an honest place for gift-idea affiliate links, without shipping two near-identical apps (which app stores can reject as spam). Every countdown is a shareable card, and it points to sister apps: payday to PocketLeft, exams to StudySprint, trips to PackRight.

## Who it's for

Anyone looking forward to something, and whoever in the household 'does the presents'.

**The problem:** People want to see the days ticking down to things they care about, and birthdays sneak up with gift ideas long forgotten.

## Version one

- Countdowns and count-ups ('days since') with themed cards
- Recurring events: payday, bin day, term dates
- People & birthdays: birthdays, anniversaries and a gift-idea note per person, captured year-round
- Reminders 2 weeks and 2 days before birthdays; budget per person and a 'bought' tick
- Share a countdown card; three free countdowns

**Example:** A worker counts down to payday and a holiday on one widget; in March they note that their sister mentioned a pottery class, and in October Daymark nudges them two weeks before her birthday with the idea and budget ready.

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Countdown hits zero: confetti cannon and the flip clock spins
- Each new day: a flip-clock digit flips with a satisfying clack
- Birthday approaching: the gift box shakes a little more each day
- Marked as bought: the ribbon unties itself into confetti
- Birthday: the cake's candles blow themselves out
