# Daymark

**Countdown to anything**

> #4 of 33 in the build order · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Plans, Trips & Occasions

## What it is

Beautiful countdowns, and 'days since' counters, for the things you're looking forward to: holidays, birthdays, payday, exams, moving day, concerts, due dates. Lives on your home screen as a widget.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Countdown apps get steady downloads, are quick to build and sell well as a lifetime unlock. Every countdown is a shareable card, and it naturally points to sister apps: payday to PocketLeft, exams to StudySprint, trips to PackRight.

## Who it's for

Broad: students, planners, parents, event-goers, anyone looking forward to something.

**The problem:** People want to see the days ticking down to the things they care about, somewhere they'll see it every day.

## Version one

- Countdowns and count-ups ('days since') with themed cards
- Days, weeks and hours, plus a progress bar
- Recurring events: payday, bin day, birthdays, term dates
- Three free countdowns
- Share a countdown card; reminder the day before

**Example:** A worker counts down to payday, a student to exams and a family to their holiday, all on one home-screen widget.

## Later

- Home-screen and lock-screen widgets
- Shared countdowns for group trips
- Photo backgrounds and premium themes

## How it makes money

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | 3 countdowns, with ads |
| Premium | £4.99–£9.99 lifetime (or £1.99/month): unlimited countdowns, widgets, themes, shared countdowns |
| Ads | Banner only, never on the countdown card |

## Why people come back

Built on **The Progression Loop**, the portfolio's shared retention framework
(streaks with forgiveness, visible progress, milestones, recaps, no guilt-trips).

| Stage | In Daymark |
|---|---|
| Cue | The countdown itself, seen daily on the widget |
| Tiny win | Glance at the widget |
| Progress | Days remaining and % complete |
| Share | Countdown cards |
| Next step | When an event arrives, prompt for the next one ('Next holiday?') |
| Cadence | Daily glance, event-driven |
